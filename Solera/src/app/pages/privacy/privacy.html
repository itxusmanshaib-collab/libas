Summary Introduction At Solera, your privacy is fundamental to our mission of delivering intelligent, safe skincare and sun protection habits trackability. This Policy outlines how we transparently manage, handle, and safeguard your personal details when utilizing our website platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you explicitly provide to us when setting up an account profile, submitting messages via our contact interfaces, or purchasing skincare items. This includes:

  • Identity Details: Full name, contact credentials, and profile usernames.
  • Contact Specifics: Valid email addresses, physical mailing addresses, and telephone sequences.
  • Skincare Metrics: Optional demographic skin traits, lifestyle routine details, and routine monitoring logs used to configure your customized platform advice profiles.

2. How We Use Your Data

Solera processes personal parameters selectively based on strict operational dependencies to achieve the following:

  • To operate individual account profiles and fulfill skincare purchases.
  • To compute accurate localized UV exposure analytics if access permissions are authorized.
  • To optimize overall user experience, interface styling, and feature configurations.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal skincare data. Data shares are restricted exclusively to accredited modern infrastructure processors required for platform operation, such as payment gateways, hosting environments, or direct product fulfillment networks.

4. Security and Storage

We employ robust data protection methods including secure HTTPS transmission, managed row-level isolation architecture, and encrypted database mechanisms to minimize intercept risks. No system is absolutely infallible, but we enforce strict precautions to keep data securely guarded.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your physical location rules (such as global privacy mandates), you retain standard administrative oversight privileges regarding your profile context:

  • The right to access, export, or audit records held in our data layers.
  • The right to modify errors or completely request profile deletion.
  • The right to immediately unsubscribe from non-essential notification campaigns.

6. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to revise this document to reflect changes in our data processing frameworks or shifting regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be declared directly here, accompanied by an updated revision date stamps.
